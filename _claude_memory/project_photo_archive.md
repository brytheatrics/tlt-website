`\\TLT-SERVER\TLT Photos\` is **155 GB** (~167 billion bytes), organized by season folder (e.g. `1213 Production Photos`). All are production photos, no raw shoots.

**Why:** A theatre's full production photo archive accumulates fast — multiple shoots per show × 25+ years.

**How to apply:** When planning what goes on the website, never propose migrating "all photos." The right pattern is keeping the full archive on TLT-SERVER as the master, and showing a curated 6-12 hero photos per show on the public site (~400 MB total site-wide). Anything more bloats hosting and slows page loads.
